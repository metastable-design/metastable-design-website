This isn't a classroom.

Textbooks teach an idealized flow. The trenches teach what actually happens when a deadline, a foundry constraint, and a stubborn timing violation collide.

Classrooms teach
  • Idealized flows with no deadline pressure
  • Textbook rules, rarely the exceptions
  • Problems sized for a semester, not a tapeout
  • Theory with no one to ask "but why does this break in practice?"
Metastable Design teaches
  • How the decision actually got made under a real schedule
  • The exceptions, the waivers, the judgment calls
  • Problems pulled from real designs, not made up for a syllabus
  • Direct access to the person who was in the room

About

Metastable Design is run by a small group of VLSI engineers who got tired of watching good design practices stay locked inside individual companies. Webinars are taught by people who've actually taped out the chips they're describing, kept small enough for real Q&A, and recorded so you can revisit the parts that didn't click the first time.
